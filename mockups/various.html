Shuffle a compilation Discogs ↗

No Spotify, Apple Music, Last.fm or Wikipedia links. Those were matched to an unrelated dubstep duo called "Various". The bio is our own line, and the photo is generated artwork, so it's the same everywhere the avatar shows.

The shelves

Shelves are worked out at build time from styles, genres and the title (Soundtrack / Stage & Screen → soundtracks; "Songs of", "Tribute to", "Reimagining" → tributes; Now, Anthems, Best of, Collected → series; the rest are anthologies). A manual override in raw_data.overrides fixes the odd miss.

Who turns up

Only 12 of 112 compilations have per-track artists today, which is why this is short. A scrapper backfill of Discogs track credits (matched to artist pages by Discogs ID, not name) fills it out, and also fixes scrobbling these records as "Various".
