Liner notes

notes on this record

Recovering the Satellites is Counting Crows' second studio album, originally released in 1996 on Geffen and produced by Gil Norton. It marked a darker, more forceful turn from the band's debut, pairing Adam Duritz's searching, often wounded lyrics with a fuller alternative rock sound on songs like "A Long December," "Angels of the Silences," and "Daylight Fading." The album was a major commercial success, reaching No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and earning multi-platinum certifications in the U.S. and Canada.

This 2026 RSD Essentials reissue on lava red vinyl brings the record back through Geffen/UMe. Its mix of aching ballads and guitar-driven momentum has kept the album a defining snapshot of mid-'90s rock — a fan favourite with lasting cultural weight.
